Who We Are

Khaled Hamad Al-Khalidi Contracting Company (KKC) is a national company based in Riyadh, specializing in General Contracting, Construction, Operation & Maintenance, and Real Estate Development.

Since our establishment, we have been committed to providing high-quality services that meet the aspirations of our clients in the government and private sectors. We rely on a qualified team of engineers and technicians, and we use the latest technologies to ensure the accuracy of execution and speed of completion.

We strive to be an effective partner in achieving the Kingdom's Vision 2030 by contributing to the development of infrastructure and urban projects with international standards.
